- **JPM (SELL 20 shares):** Financial sector reduction. May trigger gains depending on cost basis.
- **V (SELL 16 shares):** Evaluate holding period before executing.

**Tax-Loss Harvesting Opportunities:**
- Review any positions with unrealized losses for potential harvest before year-end
- If any sold positions generate losses, these can offset gains from the rebalancing

### Recommended Tax Strategy
1. **Phase the rebalancing** across two tax years if total realized gains exceed $50,000
2. **Specific lot identification:** Sell highest-cost shares first to minimize taxable gains
3. **Bond purchases** (TLT, BND) can be executed immediately as they do not trigger taxable events
4. Consider **donating appreciated shares** to charity if applicable, rather than selling
