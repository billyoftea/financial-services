## 7. Why CyberArk is NOT a Viable LBO Target

### The Fundamental Problem
1. **Growth Stock Valuation**: CyberArk trades at ~113x EBITDA because the market prices future growth. LBO returns depend on current EBITDA relative to entry price.
2. **Multiple Compression**: The entry multiple (113x) is 5x the exit multiple (22x). This massive multiple compression destroys ~80% of the entry value.
3. **Limited Debt Capacity**: With only $245M EBITDA, even 5.0x leverage provides just $1,225M in debt -- less than 5% of the $27.7B purchase price.
4. **Equity-Heavy Structure**: 95.5% equity means the PE sponsor bears nearly all the risk with minimal leverage amplification.

### What Would Make It Work
- Entry EV/EBITDA < 25x (requires ~80% stock price decline)
- Or EBITDA > $1.5B at entry (requires 6x current EBITDA)
- Or exit multiple > 80x (unrealistic for any mature company)

### PE Implications
This analysis validates the common PE practice of targeting companies with:
- EV/EBITDA of 6-12x (not 100x+)
- Stable, predictable cash flows
- Significant debt capacity relative to purchase price
- Multiple expansion or at least maintenance potential
